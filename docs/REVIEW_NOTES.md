Manic Pixie Thrift is a native shop front for an independent secondhand styling brand in Denver.

The App Clip is launched from NFC keychain fobs and QR codes that we hand out at markets and live
sales. The invocation URL is https://tap.manicpixiethrift.com/ (a QR code for it is attached: scan it
with the Camera app, or open the URL from the Notes app, to see the App Clip card).

The App Clip and the full app share one codebase and offer the same features: a gallery of the current
drop with detail pages, a countdown to the next live sale, a booking flow for styling appointments, and
links to our storefronts. No account is required, there are no ads, and no user data is collected
(privacy policy: https://tap.manicpixiethrift.com/privacy/). Content is fetched from our own domain and
ships with a bundled copy, so the app works offline.
