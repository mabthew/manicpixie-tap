Privacy

The Manic Pixie Thrift app, App Clip and this website do not collect, store or share any personal data.

The app downloads its content (text and images) anonymously from tap.manicpixiethrift.com. No account is needed, no analytics or advertising software is included, and nothing you do in the app is sent anywhere.

Buttons in the app open third-party services (Depop, Whatnot, Instagram, TikTok, Pinterest, Calendly). Those services have their own privacy policies.
